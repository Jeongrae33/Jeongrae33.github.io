01 / ABOUT

Understanding across modalities.

I am a Ph.D. student at AISTAT Lab, Chung-Ang University, advised by Prof. Changwon Lim.

My research interests are Multimodal Learning, Audio Moment Retrieval, Video Object Segmentation, and Deep Learning. I combine Mamba and DETR-style sequence models with foundation-model representations and LLM-based verification to develop efficient methods for understanding when and where events occur.
